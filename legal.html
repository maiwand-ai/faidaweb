Backtests, simulations and demo-account results are hypothetical. They have inherent limitations, do not reflect real trading with real money, and are not a guarantee of future results. Automated trading can fail because of software errors, internet or power outages, broker issues or market gaps.

Only trade with money you can afford to lose. If in doubt, seek independent financial advice.
