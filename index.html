The robots

A robot for every kind of market

Each robot reads the market first: trending, ranging or wild. It only trades when its rules say the odds are on its side.

DCA robot Trends & pullbacks

Opens when its signals agree, adds smaller trades at better prices if needed, then closes everything together at a profit.

Grid robot Sideways markets

Places buy and sell levels at fixed steps and takes a small profit each time price moves between them.

Momentum Rider Fully automatic

You choose only the lot size. It reads the 1-, 3- and 6-month trend, buys dips in uptrends and never adds to a losing trade.

AI Trader Claude or GPT

An AI model analyses each closed candle and suggests a trade. FAIDA's risk rules decide whether it's allowed and how big it is.

Profit protection

A winning trade shouldn't turn into a loss

Once a trade is well on its way to its target, FAIDA moves the stop into profit and keeps raising it as price climbs. Watch how it works.

Price Protective stop Trade opened

A stop on every trade

Every trade is sent to your broker with a stop-loss, so it's protected even if your PC or internet goes down.

Profit lock

Halfway to target, the stop moves into profit and keeps half of the gain, rising with price.

Cool-off after a loss

After a losing trade, a robot can wait before trading again instead of jumping straight back in.

Daily loss limit

See today's result against your own limit. Hit it and robots stop opening new trades.

News & session guard

Robots step aside around major economic news and thin market opens.

Emergency stop

One button closes every FAIDA trade and pauses new ones. Your own manual trades are never touched.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered honestly

    Will FAIDA make me money?

    No one can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. FAIDA gives you disciplined, tested robots with strong risk controls. Markets can still go against any strategy, and you can lose money. That's why every plan starts on a demo account.

    What do I need to run it?

    A Windows 10/11 PC or a Windows VPS that can stay on, and a MetaTrader 5 account with a broker of your choice. For 24/5 trading we recommend a small VPS.

    Does FAIDA have access to my money?

    No. FAIDA places trades on your MT5 account from your own computer. It can't deposit, withdraw or move funds, and your login details never leave your machine.

    What happens if my PC turns off?

    Every trade is sent to your broker with a stop-loss and a take-profit, so open trades stay protected. FAIDA restarts its robots on its own when your PC comes back.

    How does the AI Trader work, and does it cost extra?

    You connect your own Claude or OpenAI API key, so you pay the AI provider directly for what you use. The AI only suggests trades; FAIDA's risk rules decide whether a trade is allowed and how large it is.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel from your account page in two clicks. When a subscription ends, robots stop opening new trades but keep managing the ones already open, so nothing is left unprotected.
